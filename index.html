      Professional Image Optimization in Your Browser

      Compress Image is a comprehensive suite of tools designed to help you optimize your digital assets without compromising your privacy. Our tools leverage the power of WebAssembly to bring professional-grade compression libraries directly to your browser.

      Privacy First: Unlike other online compressors, we never upload your images to a server. All processing is done locally on your computer, ensuring your sensitive data stays private.

      Universal Image Compression

      This universal tool supports all major web image formats. Simply drop your files above, and we'll automatically detect the format and apply the most efficient compression algorithm for each file.

      Supported Formats:

      • JPEG: Best for photographs and realistic images.
      • PNG: Best for logos, icons, and images with transparency.
      • WebP: A modern alternative that offers superior compression for both lossy and lossless images.
      • AVIF: The next-generation format with even better compression than WebP.
      • GIF: For animated images and simple graphics.
      Pro Tip: You can mix and match different formats in the same batch. We'll handle everything automatically!

      How It Works

      Our tools run entirely in your browser using WebAssembly. This means:

      • Speed: No waiting for uploads or downloads.
      • Security: Your files never leave your device.
      • Reliability: Works offline once loaded.
